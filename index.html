Canine Wellness & Adopter Resources

Everything you need for a smooth transition, lifelong health, and joyful companionship.

Tailored Canine Nutrition Guide

Feeding your dog a high-protein, nutritionally balanced diet tailored to their age and weight ensures shiny coats, strong joints, and boundless energy.

  • Puppies (0-12 mo): Require calorie-dense kibble enriched with DHA for brain and eye development.
  • Adult Dogs: Portion control based on daily activity; monitor treats to prevent obesity.
  • Seniors: Add glucosamine & chondroitin supplements for joint flexibility and gentle digestion.
  • Foods to Never Feed: Chocolate, grapes/raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol sweetener, and macadamia nuts.

Hydration Rule

Dogs need approximately 1 ounce of clean water per pound of body weight daily.

Positive Reinforcement Mastery

Dogs learn fastest through clear rewards, praise, and patience. Avoid punishment-based methods that build anxiety.

  • The 3-Second Window: Reward desired behavior immediately (within 3 seconds) with high-value treats and enthusiastic verbal praise.
  • Potty Routine: Take your puppy out after waking, after meals, and after vigorous play sessions.
  • Crate as Safe Haven: Never use crates for punishment; line with plush bedding and durable chew toys.

Consistency is King

10 minutes of daily micro-training produces better results than 1 hour once a week.

Core Vaccinations & Preventative Care

All BarkHaven dogs arrive fully vaccinated, spayed/neutered, and microchipped. Here is what to keep up annually.

  • Core Vaccines: DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza) and Rabies.
  • Parasite Prevention: Monthly heartworm preventatives and flea/tick chews year-round.
  • Dental Health: Routine tooth brushing and annual dental scaling to prevent systemic organ stress.

Microchip Registry

Always keep your contact information updated with the national pet chip registry.

The 3-3-3 Rule: First Days at Home

Dogs need time to decompress and understand that they are safe in their new environment.

  • First 3 Days: Pup may feel overwhelmed, shy, or sleep extra. Keep environment quiet and predictable.
  • First 3 Weeks: Starting to settle, showing true personality, learning household rhythms and potty spots.
  • First 3 Months: Complete trust, secure bonding, feeling completely at home.
