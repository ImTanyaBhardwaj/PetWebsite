Tailored Pet Nutrition & Feeding Guide

Feeding a high-protein, nutritionally balanced diet tailored to your dog's or cat's age, weight, and breed ensures shiny coats, strong joints, and active vitality.

  • Puppies & Kittens (0–12 mo): Need calorie-dense kibble enriched with DHA for cognitive and optical growth.
  • Adult Pets: Measured portion control based on activity levels; mix dry kibbles with gravy for hydration.
  • Senior Pets (7+ yrs): Fortified with Glucosamine & Chondroitin for joint flexibility and gentle digestion.
  • Foods to Never Feed: Chocolate, onions, garlic, grapes, raisins, xylitol, caffeine, and cooked chicken bones.

Hydration Rule

Pets need approximately 50–60 ml of fresh water per kg of body weight daily.

Puppy & Kitten Daily Routine Mastery

Setting up predictable feeding, potty breaks, and playtime creates a calm, confident, and well-behaved pet.

  • Meal Schedule: Feed young puppies 3–4 small meals a day at consistent hours.
  • Potty Routine: Take your puppy out immediately after waking, after meals, and after active play.
  • Teething & Chewing: Provide calcium dental bones and rubber chew toys to protect furniture and soothe gums.

Positive Praise

Always reward good behavior within 3 seconds with high-value treats and affectionate praise.

Core Vaccinations & Preventative Healthcare

Preventative medicine safeguards your pet from life-threatening viruses and troublesome parasites.

  • Puppy Vaccinations: 6-in-1 / 7-in-1 core vaccinations at 6, 9, and 12 weeks followed by annual boosters.
  • Deworming Schedule: Every 2 weeks until 3 months, then monthly until 6 months, and every 3 months for adults.
  • Flea & Tick Prevention: Apply spot-on pipettes or use anti-tick collars especially in warm weather.
